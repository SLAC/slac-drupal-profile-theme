### iOS colour: measured, not assumed

Upstream sets an explicit `color` on the `text-button` mixin and on form fields, because iOS Safari paints both in system blue. `cssdecl.cjs` against the 5.4.6 baseline: **5 CSS files, +11 `color` declarations, nothing removed or reordered** (`alert-bar.css`'s new `color: inherit` duplicates one the same rule already sets later).

**First with upstream's `inherit` for form fields too** (`colorsweep.mjs`, all 230 stories, stage 2 vs stage 3, headless Chrome at 1280px, 1,383 elements):
- `.c-form-item__*` fields: `#000` (the UA's field text) → `#2e2d29`, inherited body text, on white: 18 rendered fields in 18 stories, plus 8 hidden ones (filter modals) in 4;
- `.c-search__form-close`, the desktop search flyout's close button: `#000` → `#2e2d29` on the white flyout (11 stories);
- the **same button inside `.c-mobile-menu`** (10 stories, not rendered): `#000` → **white**, inherited from the dark mobile menu, over a white form. Checked in the browser at 1099, 1100 and 1280px with the flyout and the mobile menu open: never visible. At ≤1099px the button is `display: none` (`breakpoint-max(mobile-menu)`), and at ≥1100px the mobile menu is;
- no other computed change.

So SLAC has no visible white-on-white case with `inherit`, unlike W6-D9 (its dark footer's newsletter input). W6-D9's value is taken anyway: its reason, that inputs are always white-backed (`$form-background-color`), holds here, so a fixed dark text colour stays right inside dark Drupal sections the stories do not cover. On every field measured, the two values give the same `#2e2d29`.
